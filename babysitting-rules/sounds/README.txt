SOUNDS

The game makes all of its own sounds, so this folder can stay empty.

Want a different sound? Put an mp3 file in this folder with one of these
names, and the game plays your file instead of its own sound:

  ambient.mp3     the quiet hum of the apartment, with a creak now and then
                  (plays over and over the whole game)
  doorbell.mp3    the doorbell: "ding-dong"
  knock.mp3       three knocks on the door
  sting.mp3       the jump-scare sound, when something suddenly appears
  heartbeat.mp3   your heartbeat while something is wrong (plays over and
                  over, and gets faster when time is running out)
  meow.mp3        the cat, outside on the balcony
  washer.mp3      the washing machine spinning (the "Spin cycle" ending)
  keys.mp3        keys in the lock when Mom and Dad come home
  click.mp3       the light switch, when the lights go out

Tips:
- The name has to match exactly: small letters, ending in .mp3.
- Reload the game page after you add a file.
- For ambient and heartbeat, pick a sound that loops smoothly.
- To get the game's own sound back, delete or rename your file.
- A good place to find free sounds is freesound.org. In the search
  filters, pick the license "Creative Commons 0" (CC0): those sounds are
  free for anyone to use. You need a free account to download, so ask a
  grown-up to help.
- Keep it creepy, not loud: no screams.
