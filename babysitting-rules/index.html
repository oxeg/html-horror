Rules for tonight

Mom & Dad

  1. By 8:30 your sister is asleep, and she doesn't wake up until morning. If she's standing in a doorway after 9, it isn't her. Don't answer when it calls your name. Go to our walk-in closet and wait until it stops.
  2. After dark, the balcony door is a mirror. If your reflection moves when you don't, walk away slowly and don't look back until you're in another room.
  3. If you see a face in the window, lock yourself in the bathroom without the washing machine and count to 100. Don't open the door for anyone, even if they sound like us.
  4. If the doorbell rings, check the camera first. If no one is there, don't answer. If the camera shows the inside of our apartment, take your sister and hide.
  5. The cat is never allowed on the balcony. If you see her out there, don't let her in. Check her tree house in our bedroom first.
  6. Keep our bedroom door closed. If you find it open, don't go in, not even to hide.
  7. We're home at 11:30. We have keys. We will never ring the doorbell.
